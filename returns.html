01Returns

Returns

You can return eligible items within 14 days of receiving your order. Items must be unused, unaltered, and in their original packaging with all tags attached.

02Refunds

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you about the status of your refund. If approved, the refund will be issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Eligibility

  • Products must be in original condition
  • Not worn, washed, or damaged
  • All tags and packaging must be included
  • Sale items and limited drops may not be eligible

How to return

  • Contact our support team with your order number.

  • Pack the item securely.
  • Send the package using a tracked shipping method.
